Compress images online to make photos and pictures lighter while keeping them clear enough for web pages, sharing, and everyday use. Squishyfile works in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

## Quick Steps

1. Choose an image from your device or drag it into the upload area.
2. Select an output format: JPG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF.
3. Choose a quality level: 40%, 60%, 75%, 85%, or 95%.
4. Set a width if you want to resize the image.
5. Download the smaller image and check the result before replacing the original.

## When to Use This Image Compressor

Use this tool when you want to reduce the file size of photos, screenshots, product pictures, blog images, email attachments, or images that feel too large to upload or share.

For most images, WebP with 75% or 85% quality is a good starting point. Use JPG for broad compatibility, PNG when you need sharp graphics or transparency, WebP for smaller modern web images, and AVIF when you want very small files and your target platform supports it.

## Quality and Width Tips

Lower quality creates smaller files, but may make details look softer. Higher quality keeps more detail, but the file will be larger.

If the image is a large camera photo, reducing the width often saves more space than lowering quality too much. For web pages and blog images, you usually do not need the original full camera resolution.
