Compress JPG and JPEG images online to make photos smaller while keeping them clear for websites, email, uploads, and everyday sharing. Squishyfile works in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

## Quick Steps

1. Choose a JPG or JPEG image from your device or drag it into the upload area.
2. Choose a quality level: 40%, 60%, 75%, 85%, or 95%.
3. Set a width if you want to resize a large photo.
4. Download the compressed JPG and compare it with the original.
5. Keep the original file if you may need the full-quality version later.

## When to Use This JPG Compressor

Use this tool when you want to reduce the file size of JPG photos, camera images, product pictures, blog images, and JPEG files that are too large to upload or send.

JPG compression works best for photos and colorful real-world images. For most photos, 75% or 85% quality is a good starting point because it can make the file much smaller without making the image look obviously damaged.

## Quality and Photo Tips

Lower quality creates a smaller JPG file, but too much compression can add blur, blocky areas, or rough edges around details.

If the original photo is very large, reducing the width can save more space than lowering quality too far. This is useful for website images, blog photos, thumbnails, and email attachments that do not need full camera resolution.
