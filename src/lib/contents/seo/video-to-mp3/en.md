Use Squishyfile to extract audio from a video file and save it as an MP3 directly in your browser. Choose a video from your device, convert the audio, and download the MP3 without installing software or creating an account.

## How to Convert a Video to MP3

Converting a video to MP3 only takes a few steps. The tool reads the audio track inside your video file and creates a separate MP3 file from it.

### Step 1: Choose your video file

Drag and drop your video into the upload area, or click to select a file from your device. You can use videos from your phone, screen recordings, meeting recordings, exported video projects, or other local files.

Supported formats include MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI, and many other common video files. The video must contain an audio track. If the original video has no sound, there will be no audio to extract.

### Step 2: Convert the audio in your browser

After selecting the file, start the conversion. Squishyfile processes the video in your browser and converts the audio track into MP3.

Keep the browser tab open while the file is being processed. For long videos or very large files, conversion may take more time, especially on a phone or older device.

### Step 3: Download the MP3

When the conversion is complete, download the MP3 file to your device. You can play it in music players, file apps, media players, cloud storage apps, or audio editing tools that support MP3.

The downloaded file contains the audio only. It will not include the video image, subtitles, slides, or other visual content from the original video.

Because the conversion happens on your own device, performance depends on your browser, device memory, file size, and video length. Smaller files usually finish faster, while long videos may need more time.

## Before You Convert

A few checks can help you get a better result:

- play the original video first to confirm it has sound
- check that the audio is clear enough before converting
- avoid converting damaged or incomplete video files
- use a computer for very large or long videos
- remember that MP3 keeps audio only, not video, slides, or subtitles
- do not repeatedly convert the same audio if you want to preserve quality

MP3 is a compressed audio format. It is not lossless, but it is widely supported, easy to play, and practical for everyday listening, study notes, meeting reviews, and simple audio storage.
