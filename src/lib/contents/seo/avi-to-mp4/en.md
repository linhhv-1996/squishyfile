## When to convert AVI to MP4

AVI is an older container format common in files from Windows PCs, older cameras, and legacy recording software. It may not open reliably on modern phones, browsers, and social platforms. Converting AVI to MP4 makes the file easier to play, share, and import into editing apps.

Common reasons to convert AVI to MP4:

- Play older footage on an iPhone, Android, or modern smart TV
- Share a video by chat, email, or cloud storage without compatibility issues
- Upload footage to a platform that does not accept AVI
- Import an archived recording into current video editing software

## AVI to MP4 on any device

The converter works in modern browsers on Windows, Mac, iPhone, and Android. On mobile, open the page in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and select a video from your device.

AVI files from older cameras tend to be large and may use legacy codecs. If conversion is slow or fails on mobile, use a desktop browser instead. Keep the screen active and do not close the tab while the file is being processed.

If the source AVI does not play in any media player, the file may be damaged or encoded with a codec the browser cannot read.
