# How to Compress Video for Discord Under 10MB

Discord is great for sending short clips, but the upload limit can block even a few seconds of phone video or gameplay.

For free Discord accounts, the safest target is **9MB**. That leaves a small buffer under the 10MB limit, so the upload is less likely to fail after compression.

## Best Discord Compression Settings

Use these targets depending on your account:

| Discord account | Upload limit | Safe target size |
|---|---:|---:|
| Free | 10MB | 9MB |
| Nitro Basic | 50MB | 49MB |
| Nitro | 500MB | 490MB |

If you are not sure which limit applies to you, use **9MB**. It is the safest option for sending a video in any normal Discord chat without Nitro.

## How to Compress a Video for Discord

1. Open the video compressor.
2. Drop in your video file.
3. Choose **Target File Size**.
4. Enter **9** if you are using a free Discord account.
5. Compress and download the smaller MP4.
6. Upload the compressed file to Discord.

Using a target size is better than guessing with a general quality preset because Discord has a hard upload limit. If the output is 10.2MB, Discord may still reject it. A 9MB target gives you room for file metadata and platform differences.

## Which Preset Should You Use?

For Discord, use this order:

- **Target File Size**: best when you must stay under 10MB.
- **Small File**: useful for quick clips when exact size does not matter.
- **Balanced**: good for Nitro users or larger limits.
- **High Quality**: only use this if your account allows larger uploads.

For free Discord users, **Target File Size → 9MB** is the most reliable setting.

## Why Discord Videos Get Too Large

Most videos are recorded at a much higher quality than Discord needs.

A short video can become large because of:

- 1080p or 4K resolution
- 60fps recording
- screen recording at full display size
- fast gameplay or camera movement
- high video bitrate
- MOV files from iPhone
- clips exported from editing apps at high quality

Discord does not care how short the video feels. It only checks the final file size. A 12-second gameplay clip can fail if it is exported at a high bitrate.

## What File Type Works Best on Discord?

Use **MP4**.

MP4 is the safest format for Discord previews, mobile playback, and desktop playback. If your original file is MOV, AVI, MKV, or WebM, compressing it to MP4 usually makes it easier to upload and play.

Squishyfile outputs MP4, so you do not need to convert the video separately.

## How to Keep Quality Acceptable Under 10MB

A 10MB limit is tight, especially for long or fast-moving videos. To keep the result watchable:

- Trim the clip before compressing.
- Keep only the part you actually want to send.
- Avoid sending long 1080p or 4K clips under 10MB.
- Use 9MB for short clips, not long videos.
- If the video has fast motion, consider sending a link instead.

For short clips, Discord-sized compression is usually fine. For long gameplay recordings, event videos, or high-quality edits, 10MB may be too small to preserve good detail.

## When Compression Is Not the Best Option

Compress the video if:

- the clip is short
- you want to upload directly in Discord
- quality only needs to look good in chat
- you do not want to use a cloud link

Use a link instead if:

- the video is several minutes long
- the file needs to stay high quality
- the clip contains small text or UI details
- it is a full gameplay recording
- you are sharing with many people

Compression is best for quick sharing. Links are better for large videos where quality matters.

## Compress Discord Videos on iPhone

1. Open the compressor in Safari.
2. Select your video from Photos.
3. Set the target size to **9MB**.
4. Compress the video.
5. Save the MP4 to Files or Photos.
6. Upload it in Discord.

This is useful for iPhone MOV clips, which are often much larger than needed for Discord.

## Compress Discord Videos on Android

1. Open the compressor in Chrome.
2. Choose a video from Gallery or Files.
3. Enter **9MB** as the target size.
4. Compress and download.
5. Upload the MP4 to Discord.

The downloaded file is usually saved in your Downloads folder.

## Common Problems

### Discord still says the file is too large

Compress again with a smaller target, such as **8MB**. Some files can end up slightly above the target depending on the source video.

### The video looks blurry

The target size is probably too small for the video length. Trim the video or use a larger limit if you have Nitro.

### The video has no preview

Use MP4 output. Discord handles MP4 more reliably than formats like MOV, AVI, or MKV.

### The upload fails on mobile

Try uploading from desktop, or make the target slightly smaller. Mobile apps can be stricter when handling large media files.

## FAQ

**What size should I use for Discord free accounts?**  
Use **9MB**. It leaves a safe buffer under the 10MB upload limit.

**Can I compress a video to exactly 10MB?**  
You can try, but 9MB is safer. A file that is exactly at the limit may still fail depending on metadata or how Discord reads the file.

**Does Discord support MP4?**  
Yes. MP4 is the best format to use for Discord video uploads.

**Can I compress gameplay clips for Discord?**  
Yes, but keep them short. Gameplay has fast motion, so it needs more bitrate to stay sharp.

**Should I use compression or a cloud link?**  
Use compression for short clips. Use a link for long videos or anything where quality matters.
